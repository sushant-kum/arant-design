ARANT DESIGN · Identity kit · v1.1

Two equal stone slabs lean together and hold a pebble between them. The A becomes an object in balance: architectural, tactile and quiet.

Objects for Considered Spaces.

Every opening in the symbol, the split between the slabs and the gap around the pebble, has the same width. That one rule is what lets the mark survive a rubber stamp, a blind deboss and a silicone mould.

02 · Construction

One opening, repeated

Split
x = 5 % of the symbol's grid. The two slabs are mirror images.
Pebble gap
Also x, measured square to each slab's inner edge, not horizontally.
Tops and feet
Fully rounded caps and softened feet: no point narrower than the split, nothing to chip in a mould.
Wordmark
ARANT is drawn, not typed. Both As are the symbol itself, scaled to the cap height. R, N and T share its stroke (about 18 % of the cap height) and its corner radius. The N's diagonal sits at 60°.
Descriptor
DESIGN is set at 27 % of the ARANT cap height and spaced to the exact width of ARANT.
03 · Clear space and minimum size

Room to breathe, openings that stay open

Clear space on every side is half the ARANT cap height (for the symbol alone, a quarter of its height). Minimum sizes follow one production rule: keep every opening at 0.8 mm or more.

Widths are measured on the artwork, not the file's margin. The narrowest opening in every lockup is the split in the As of ARANT. These are production rules of thumb. Before the first run, make one proof rubber stamp and one test pour at the smallest size you plan to use.

05 · Typography

A geometric sans and an editorial serif

The logo is custom-drawn and never typed. For everything around it, use two open-licence families from Google Fonts. Both are under the SIL Open Font License, free for commercial use, print and web.

Primary · Jost

Objects for Considered Spaces.

Geometric, like the wordmark. Use it for labels, navigation, product details, prices and packaging copy. Regular 400 and Medium 500; set uppercase labels with +0.2em tracking.

Secondary · Newsreader

Cast by hand, finished slowly.

Editorial serif for headlines, product names, stories and cards. Light 300 for display, Regular 400 for text. Never in all caps.

07 · Pattern

The symbol as texture

For tissue paper, box liners and the website background, repeat the symbol on a loose grid and turn every second one upside down, in Sand on Ivory or Ivory on Sand. Keep it at least three times smaller than any logo on the same surface, so it reads as texture, not as a second logo.

08 · Don'ts

Keep the balance

Also: don't retype ARANT in a font, don't rearrange or resize the parts of a lockup, and don't place the logo on busy photography without a calm area or a solid panel.
