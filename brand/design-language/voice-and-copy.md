# Voice and copy

How ARANT writes. Back to the [design-language index](README.md).

---

## What is established

**Established** (identity README; guide mockups):

- Tagline: **Objects for Considered Spaces.** Always in title case with the full stop; the capitals replace the case
  only where a design sets it in uppercase (a Jost label, the seal's ring text). Decided by the studio; this resolves
  the earlier open decision (see the [index](README.md#open-decisions)).
- Thank-you card: "Thank you for giving this object a place in your home."
- Instagram bio line: "Small-batch, hand-finished in India."
- Example product name and collection: "Plinth Tray", "The Plinth collection"; prices written `₹1,800`.
- British English (`cspell.json`).

**Positioning statement** (the studio's brief for this design system; not a tagline, and not for use as a headline):
_A contemporary Indian objects and home-design brand creating refined objects for considered spaces._ Use it to
brief people, in press notes and "about" copy, where a full sentence is needed.

## The voice

**Recommended:** thoughtful, clear, concise, confident, warm, contemporary, understated.

| We are            | So we                                                      | Not                                           |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Thoughtful        | Explain why a form or material was chosen                  | Sentimental or precious                       |
| Clear and concise | Use short sentences and plain words; one idea per sentence | Clever for its own sake; puns                 |
| Confident         | State facts and let the object speak                       | Hype, superlatives, "luxury", "exclusive"     |
| Warm and human    | Write to one person, about their home and daily use        | Corporate ("solutions", "curated offerings")  |
| Understated       | Use one precise adjective rather than three                | "Stunning", "perfect", "must-have", "elevate" |

**Write about:** form, material, object, space, use, process, detail, intention.

**Avoid:** exaggerated luxury claims, generic e-commerce phrases ("shop now", "don't miss out"), fake scarcity,
exclamation marks, emoji in brand copy, corporate jargon, and sentimental lines about "soul" or "stories woven".

## Rules

- **Material and process at product level only.** "Cast", "poured", "turned", "thrown" belong in a product's own
  description, never in brand-level lines, packaging frames or the site's navigation.
- **Truthful.** Only say "handmade", "hand-finished" or "small-batch" when it is true of that product. State that
  hand-finished pieces vary, plainly, as a fact rather than an apology.
- **Specific over general.** "32 × 18 cm, 1.2 kg" beats "generously sized".
- **Sentence case** for headlines and buttons in running text; uppercase only in Jost labels (see
  [`typography.md`](typography.md#casing)).
- **Numbers and units:** `32 × 18 cm` (multiplication sign, spaces), `1.2 kg`, `₹1,800`.
- **British English:** colour, centre, finish, grey, organise.

## Naming

**Recommended:**

- **Collections** take a single, plain noun from architecture, landscape or form: the Plinth collection. Short,
  easy to say in Indian and international English, not a made-up word.
- **Products** are the collection name plus the object: Plinth Tray, Plinth Coaster. Colour or finish follows as an
  option, not in the name: Plinth Tray, Sand.
- No material in collection names (so a collection can span materials), no numbers or codes in public names.

## Examples

All names and prices below are examples.

### Product titles

- Plinth Tray
- Plinth Candle Holder, Sand
- Threshold Vase (a hypothetical ceramic)

### Product descriptions

> A low tray with the weight of stone, sized for keys, a watch and the things you set down at the door. Each piece is
> finished by hand, so its surface varies slightly from the one pictured.

> A tall vase with a narrow opening that holds a single stem upright. Thrown in stoneware and left unglazed outside,
> so the clay stays soft to the touch.

### Collection introductions

> **The Plinth collection.** Low, solid forms that lift everyday things off the surface and give them a place.

> **Threshold.** Objects for the edges of a room: the entrance table, the window ledge, the step.

### Instagram captions

> The Plinth Tray, by the door. Keys, a watch, the day's small things.

> A detail of the edge, sanded by hand until it is soft to the touch.

> New in the studio: the Plinth collection. Four objects for the surfaces you use every day. arantdesign.com

### Packaging copy

> Thank you for giving this object a place in your home. _(Established, thank-you card)_

> Wipe with a soft, damp cloth. Keep away from standing water.

> Designed in India by ARANT DESIGN.
